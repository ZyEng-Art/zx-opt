General-phase ZX rules and nonlinear JPV (A)

Implemented scope
- --general-zx enables the 12 general JPV schemata S/I/CP/B/K/EU/H/SUP/E/C/BW/A.
  It implies --jpv and --allow-arbitrary-angles. The previous strict CT and
  conditional mixed-angle modes remain available.
- General S/H/K/SUP/C use arbitrary supported real phase expressions. Constants
  in the other rules retain the literal JPV templates. S/H have no mathematical
  arity cap in the kernel; the finite proposal interface is separately bounded.
- A is transcribed from Figure 3 of https://arxiv.org/pdf/1903.06035v3 .
  The relevant completeness theorem is Theorem 1.3, not the CT Theorem 1.1.
  Both colors, directions, general open contexts and scalar leaves are retained.

Exact phase and rule records
- Rational coefficients of pi keep their historical Fraction representation.
  Nonrational angles use an exact real expression tree backed by SymPy.
  For example acos(3/4)/pi is retained rather than rounded into a pi fraction.
- Real symbols and supported exact operations can be serialized as JSON ASTs.
  No eval/sympify is applied to external strings. Unbound symbols are allowed
  in the rule kernel, but must be bound before numerical circuit scoring.
- MeanArg/MeanAcos implement the total real polar solution of (A), in units of
  pi, with arg(0)=0. They require four real input arguments. The unit-disc bound
  follows from the triangle inequality and |cos(alpha)| <= 1.
- The named axiom, phase domain, expression AST and constraint evidence are
  reconstructed and checked again when a saved action is replayed.
- C8 exact_tensor remains restricted to pi/4 phases and rejects expressions.

Nonlinear side condition and certificates
All angles below are in radians:
  2 exp(i theta3) cos(gamma)
    = exp(i theta1) cos(alpha) + exp(i theta2) cos(beta).
Forward construction sets z to half the right side, theta3=arg(z) and
gamma=acos(abs(z)); z=0 uses theta3=0 and gamma=pi/2.

Executable instances currently accept:
1. The constructive polar certificate above.
2. The diagonal inverse: alpha=beta=gamma, theta1=theta2=theta3.
3. The symmetric inverse: alpha=beta=0, theta1=theta3+gamma,
   theta2=theta3-gamma.
4. Other instances whose side condition SymPy proves as an exact identity.

A small numeric residual is never an admissibility certificate. A failed
constraint is classified as disproved or unknown; neither may execute. The CAS
fallback has a work/complexity bound and is not a complete real-expression
equality decision procedure.

Literal tensor coverage
The Figure 3 transcription has the following Z-colored open tensors:
  L = (1+i, exp(i*pi/4) *
       [exp(i*theta1)*cos(alpha) + exp(i*theta2)*cos(beta)])
  R = (1+i, 2*exp(i*(theta3+pi/4))*cos(gamma)).
Thus the side condition implies equal tensors without changing amplitudes or
dropping scalar factors. Color duality applies H to both tensors. The verification
tool independently contracts both sides symbolically and checks these formulas
for real symbols, in addition to finite numeric checks.

Bounded proposals and PPO
- Topology is matched first. Source phase labels determine the affine parameters
  exactly modulo 2*pi. This replaces the eight-value phase signature lookup in
  general mode. SUP backward retains both roots.
- A forward solves its four source parameters. A backward offers the two exact
  inverse families above. These finite proposals do not enumerate every member
  of the continuous inverse family.
- The policy chooses finite discrete actions. The rule solver computes angles;
  the network does not need to regress continuous output angles.
- Existing node/edge/candidate/matching/horizon bounds remain. General proposals
  also reject templates with more than 128 symbolic operations in any phase.
  This is a proposal resource bound, not a restriction of the axiom factory.
- New A action embeddings are enabled only for general-mode models; the default
  model architecture and old action indices are retained.

Scoring representation
- The symbolic raw diagram remains the proof/search state. Numeric export of
  expressions is explicit, with 40 decimal digits of phase coefficient, solely
  for the independently verified scoring view.
- When a raw phase is symbolic, native/macro transitions through that numeric
  view are disabled so they cannot overwrite the exact expression state.
- Graph normalization, extraction, output QASM, named wire order and amplitude
  checks retain the existing full-operator checks at tolerance 2e-8.
- The raw cache key retains expression ASTs. Numerically close expressions are
  not merged. Legal unscorable graphs still use the separate latent pool.

The complete apply command also accepts --general-zx and --axiom A. Its JSON
parameters use fractions in units of pi or the expression AST. Import accepts
--allow-arbitrary-angles; export requires --evaluate-expressions for a symbolic
diagram and is explicitly a numeric view, not an exact symbolic export.

Completeness boundary
The registry represents the published general JPV axiom schemata. The theorem
concerns the unrestricted mathematical calculus. Our executable expression and
side-condition certificate language is specified above; it is not a proof that
all equal real expressions can be decided automatically. Unknown constraints
are rejected. Finite RL proposals/search are not complete and extraction is not
guaranteed. No optimization or learning improvement follows just from adding A.
