General-angle multi-circuit study, 2026-10-17

Targets: barenco_tof_3 and mod5_4 (existing NAM/Reversible benchmarks), plus
qft_4_numeric and ising_4_numeric (deterministic synthetic 4-qubit circuits
generated by tools/make_angle_inputs.py). The synthetic circuits are functional
inputs, not previously discovered optimized witnesses. QASM parser output is
the numerical reference; no symbolic original-circuit exactness is claimed.

For every circuit: conditional-angle JPV versus --general-zx; each with frozen
and online policy, seeds 11/29/47, 600 seconds per trial including matching,
feasibility, verification, extraction and PPO. Total 48 trials. Each target has
its own model/archive. No held-out-circuit generalization evaluation.

Within-mode frozen/online pairs have identical random initial weights and
first 128-step rollout. Across modes action embeddings differ; comparisons
measure the whole configured system and do not isolate A's causal effect.
Both modes accept arbitrary numeric angles. Conditional mode restricts JPV
internal matching to pi/4; general mode includes affine matching and A.

128-step/one-update preflight for all eight mode/circuit configurations.
All seeds/arms retained, including regressions. Afterwards replay every
accepted rewrite, check every saved QASM against the fixed-wire operator,
audit source/input hashes and frozen/online model pairing, and probe policy
changes on a fixed root state. Inspect A selection, extraction recovery,
throughput, reward density, loss, entropy and value explained variance.

Node44 CPU-only container, at most 48 single-thread search workers, no GPU.
Immutable clean Git source snapshot; stop only the exact container incarnation
started by this study. Preserve raw logs, models, states and proofs in archive.
